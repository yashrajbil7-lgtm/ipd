About

      
Project: Indian Classical Music Ornament Detection

      
Goal: The system detects musical ornaments from audio recordings using machine learning.

      
Detected Ornaments:

      
    
        
  • Meend – a smooth glide from one note to another
    • 
        
  • Kanswar – a brief touch of a neighbouring grace note
    • 
        
  • Murki – a quick, light cluster of two or three notes
    •
